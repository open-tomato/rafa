title: Planned under assumptions
level: minor

- Claims: an issue is now claimed in git through its pushed `feat/<stub>` branch, whose empty claim commits name the owning store id, so no two devices can plan or start the same issue; new `rafa claim release <n>`, `rafa claim hand <n> --to=<store id> [--withdraw]`, `rafa claim accept <n>` and `rafa claim take <n> [--stale]` move ownership with one lease-checked push each and start no session; a claimant whose push is refused because another device took the claim keeps its commits on a local `lost/<stub>` branch and gets a "claim lost" report, never a force-push; `rafa switch` and every second `rafa loop start` report (never fix) where ticked checklist lines, claim labels and claim branches disagree.
- Plan: `rafa plan create` claims its issue before the planning session: a claim held by another device refuses `--issue` with exit 1 naming the owner, `--next` passes that issue and walks on, a released or stale `rafa:claimed` branch is taken over, and a claim that cannot be made or pushed (no remote, an NDJSON store or an unminted store id) writes the plan with an "unclaimed" warning; `--claim-ahead` (or `claims.ahead: allow`) also claims the next line of the same board in one atomic push, both or neither, and says when that line is on another board.
- Loop: `rafa loop start` pushes a claim `plan create` could not push, refuses to run an issue another device owns (naming its store id) or while the claim cannot be pushed, and moves the issue's label from `rafa:claimed` to `rafa:in-development`; a wrap-up push refused because the claim was taken halts with the claim lost report and opens no pull request.
- Config: new `claims.staleAfter` (whole hours or days, or `disabled`; default `3d`; 0 or below refused) and `claims.ahead` (`off | allow`, default `off`), both declared as locked settings.
- Board: `rafa next` and the roadmap and epic readings treat a released claim as free, offer a stale `rafa:claimed` claim for takeover and keep a stale `rafa:in-development` claim taken with a stale note; `rafa init --board` creates the `rafa:claimed` and `rafa:in-development` labels and `rafa doctor` reports them when missing; `rafa next --roadmap --claim-ahead` passes the flag to its plan step.
- Status: `rafa status` adds a Claims section listing each claim branch with its owning store, its stage label, whether it is stale and the `rafa claim take` line that would take it over.
- CLI: the `claim` subject and its four actions appear in `rafa --help` and `rafa describe`.
- Docs: `context/cli.md` documents the `claim` subject, `--claim-ahead` and the claim steps of `plan create` and `loop start`; `context/pull-requests.md` gains a "Claims" section; the README gives the claims config lightest first.
- Tests: spawned tests over a bare remote and two real clones hold a racing `plan create` landing one claim with no session for the loser, an offline plan claimed later by `loop start`, release, handover, withdrawal and takeover including a lease-refused race, the claim lost halt at wrap-up, claim ahead's all-or-nothing push, a roadmap walk over held, released and stale claims, and `rafa switch` reporting drift with no `gh` write.
